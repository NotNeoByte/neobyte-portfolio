        Commission Workflow

        01 / 03

        How an order goes from your first message to the finished files.

        1. 01
          Open a ticket

          Join my Discord and open a commission ticket. Include references, size or scale, style, your deadline and your budget.

        2. 02
          Quote & timeline

          I reply with a price and a delivery estimate. Small jobs start at 5,000 R$ or $19. Need it sooner? Rush is $50 per day skipped.

        3. 03
          50% deposit

          Work starts once the first half is paid, in Robux or USD. Your order then gets a spot in my queue.

        4. 04
          Build & progress updates

          I post progress screenshots in your ticket so you can give feedback early, while changes are still easy.

        5. 05
          Final payment & delivery

          When the work is done, you pay the remaining 50% and I hand over the files (model, place file or UI files).

        6. 06
          Revisions

          You get 3 free small revisions, requested within 48 hours of delivery.

        By paying the deposit, you agree to the Terms of Service.

        Terms of Service

        02 / 03

        These terms apply to every commission. Paying the deposit means you accept them.

        1.Payment

        Prices are in Robux or USD. You pay 50% upfront before work starts, and the remaining 50% on completion, before the files are delivered.

        2.Revisions

        You get 3 free small revisions (colors, part placement, minor details). They must be requested within 48 hours of delivery.

        Large changes, changes outside the original brief, and requests made after the 48 hours are charged extra.

        3.Rush orders

        Rush delivery costs $50 for each day skipped from the standard timeline, depending on my queue.

        4.Team Create

        I only work in your Team Create place if the order is paid 100% upfront. Otherwise I build in my own place and deliver the files.

        5.Percentage payment

        I don't take a percentage of game revenue as payment unless the game already has 10M+ visits, 3k+ active players and decent revenue.

        6.Cancellations & refunds

        • If you cancel after work has started, the deposit isn't refunded.
        • If I can't finish the order, you're refunded for the work that wasn't done.

        7.Usage rights

        • After full payment, you can use the work in your own Roblox games.
        • You can't resell or redistribute the files as assets without my permission.
        • I may show the work in my portfolio and on my socials, unless we agree otherwise before I start.

        8.Chargebacks

        Reversing a payment after delivery cancels your usage rights, and you won't be able to commission me again.

        9.General

        • Orders are handled through Discord tickets.
        • I can decline any commission.
        • If these terms change, the version in place when you paid your deposit applies to your order.

        Privacy Policy

        03 / 03

        This site is a portfolio. It has no visitor accounts, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

        1.What this site collects

        Browsing the site stores nothing about you. If you post a review, the site keeps the name, optional Roblox username, rating and text you enter. Reviews are checked before they appear publicly.

        2.What I keep during a commission

        To handle your order, I keep your Discord username, the brief and files you send, and payment records. I use them only for your order and never sell or share them.

        3.Payments

        Payments go through Roblox or the payment provider you use. I never see your card or bank details.

        4.Third-party services

        Reviews are stored with Google Firebase. Contact links go to Discord and X. Their own privacy policies apply.

        5.Deleting your info

        Ask in a Discord ticket to have your review, brief or files deleted. Payment records are kept in case of disputes.
