Commission Workflow

Last update: March 15, 2026

What Are Commissions?

Roblox commissions are paid requests where someone hires a developer (in this case, me aka NeoByte) to make custom work for their Roblox project. This includes things like Building and 3D Modeling. The client pays Robux or real money in exchange for the completed work.

How the process first starts with?

A Roblox commission usually starts when a client contacts me via my DM/creating a ticket from my Discord Portfolio Server with a request for work. They explain what they need (like models, building etc.), discuss the price and deadline, and once both agree on the details and client pays the upfront, I (developer) begins working on the commission.

How the entire Commission works?

  • Work in Progress (WIP): I share progress updates every 16-24hr so the client can review the work.
  • Feedback & Revisions: The client gives feedback and requests small changes if needed.
  • Final Delivery: Once the work is finished and approved, the creator sends the final files and receives the agreed payment.

How does a commission end?

A commission typically ends when the client is satisfied with the final result. After that, the remaining payment is given by the client to the developer, and then the developer shares all the necessary files!! Sometimes it is followed by a review or testimonial.

How does Revisions (after commission) carried out?

After the client receives the file, they review it in their game/project and note any changes needed. They send clear feedback (often with screenshots or explanations) within 48hr of me sending the file (if time exceeds, extra charges will be issued), and I update the work and resend the revised version. This continues until the free revision limit (3 in this case) is reached. Any additional changes cost extra, and the 3 changes must be small — not a prior re-work from scratch, else extra charges apply.

How can I review NeoByte's Service?

I have a special Vouch Server where you can review my service — or just use the Reviews page right here on this site.

Terms of Service

Last update: March 15, 2026

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your use of NeoByte's Commission services. By hiring me, you agree to comply with these Terms and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to these Terms, please do not hire me.

1. Payment

  • Minimum payment is $20 USD or 5,000 Robux.
  • If paying via Roblox gamepass, the client must cover tax.
  • 50% upfront payment is required before work begins (non-negotiable).
  • Remaining 50% must be paid before final delivery.
  • No percentage-based payments are accepted.

2. Process

  • Work will begin only after upfront payment is received.
  • Progress updates (WIP) will be provided every 24 hours.
  • Clients are expected to respond in a reasonable timeframe for feedback.

3. Revisions

  • You are entitled to 3 free minor revisions after delivery.
  • Revisions must be requested within 48 hours of receiving the files.
  • Additional or late revisions will require extra payment.
  • Major changes (redesigns, new features, large edits) are not considered revisions and are charged separately.

4. Delivery

  • Final files will only be delivered after 100% payment is completed.
  • Only .rbxm / .rblx files will be provided.
  • Source files (.blend, .obj, .fbx, etc.) will not be shared unless it's a UGC Commission.

5. Client Responsibility

  • The client must provide clear instructions and requirements before work begins.
  • The client must remain professional and respectful during the commission.
  • Do not spam for updates — they're sent every 24hr.

6. Delays & Inactivity

  • If the client is unresponsive for an extended period, the commission may be paused or cancelled.
  • Delays caused by the client may affect the final delivery timeline.

7. Cancellation

  • I reserve the right to cancel a commission at any time.
  • Once upfront payment is received, the order cannot be cancelled by either party.

8. Refund Policy

  • No refunds will be given for issues caused by the client.
  • If I choose to issue a refund, only the amount received by me will be refunded.
  • I am not responsible for covering tax, and a portion may be deducted based on work completed.

9. Usage Rights

  • The client is allowed to use the work for their project.
  • Reselling or redistributing the work is not allowed unless agreed otherwise.
  • I retain the right to showcase the work in my portfolio and claim that it's made by me even after the client pays in full.

10. Disputes & Chargebacks

  • Any attempt at chargebacks or payment disputes will result in blacklisting and possible reporting.
